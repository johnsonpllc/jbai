01This Week's Tip

Main Tip: Turn a Quiet Tuesday Into a Full House With Smart Text-Back Campaigns

A single well-timed text can do more for your slow nights than a week of social media posts.

  • Capture opt-ins at checkout or on receipts, then let AI segment guests by visit frequency, order type, and last visit date.
  • Send a personalized Tuesday-specific offer within 48 hours of a visit, while your restaurant is still fresh in their mind.
  • Keep messages short and direct: a real offer, a real deadline, a real reason to come back this week.
  • According to Klaviyo, SMS open rates average around 98%, making text the highest-attention channel you have.
  • Use AI to auto-pause messages for guests who book, so no one feels spammed after they say yes.
  • Track redemption by day-part to refine your Tuesday offer over time without guesswork.
