01This Week's Tip

Main Tip: The 3-Paragraph System Prompt That Gives Your AI a Real Personality

Stop letting your AI sound like everyone else's AI.

  • Paragraph one is your voice guide: write 2-3 sentences describing how you actually talk, your tone, and what you never say.
  • Paragraph two is your context: tell it your role, your audience, and what you are trying to help people accomplish.
  • Paragraph three is your rules: list specific dos and don'ts, like "always use short sentences" or "never say the word leverage."
  • Read your prompt out loud before saving it. If it sounds stiff, your AI will sound stiff.
  • Pull real phrases from your own past writing and paste them directly into the prompt as style examples.
  • Test it immediately with something you would normally write yourself, then compare the output side by side.
